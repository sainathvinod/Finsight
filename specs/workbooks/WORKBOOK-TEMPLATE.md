# DRAFT — Approval Workbook (Template) (Gate 2)

> **DRAFT TEMPLATE — AI-prepared shape only. No approvals recorded.** The real workbook is generated by `/sdd:workbook` over the migrated `specs/` registers (Gate 2, step 3) and **signed by a human**. AI must never sign it (Constitution §5, DEC-034). Copy to `specs/workbooks/WORKBOOK-<yyyy-mm-dd>.md` and fill.

- **Purpose:** capture the human, per-item Approve/Disapprove decision for every baselined requirement/epic/feature/rule, in business language, as the approval evidence the baseline (Gate 2) requires.
- **Required human approvers:** **Project Owner** (accountable); **Amerisure & finance stakeholders** consulted on requirements/rules; **Finance Governance Lead** on rule intent.
- **Evidence required:** completed workbook with a Decision + approver per in-scope item; a signature/date block; stored at `specs/workbooks/WORKBOOK-<date>.md`.
- **Acceptance criteria:** no in-scope item left blank; every item Approved (or Disapproved with a reason routed to a CR); the workbook is signed; `/sdd:baseline` accepts it as approval evidence.

**How to populate:** after Gate-2 migration, `/sdd:workbook` enumerates all items from `specs/01`, `specs/02`, `specs/03`, `specs/04`, `specs/05`. One row per item. Placeholders (`{FR-###}`) below show the shape only.
