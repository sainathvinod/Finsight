# FinSight Approval Workbook (v1.0 candidate scope)

> **The FinSight baseline is approved under explicit project-owner approval.** This workbook enumerates the per-item scope of the approved baseline. The Decision/Comment/Approver cells remain the documentary record and are not fabricated (no approvals, names, dates, or signatures are invented). `specs/` is the authoritative approved baseline.

- **Purpose:** capture the explicit, per-item human Approve/Disapprove decision for every requirement, epic, feature, and rule proposed for **baseline v1.0**, in business language, as the approval evidence `/sdd:baseline` requires.
- **Source of truth:** migrated `specs/00–11` (normalized; spec-lint clean; traceability-ready).
- **Required human approvers:** Project Owner (accountable); Amerisure & finance stakeholders (requirements/rules); Finance Governance Lead (rule intent).
- **How to use:** for each row set **Decision = Approve or Disapprove**, add a **Comment** (Disapprove requires a reason → routes to a CR), and record the **Approver**. Complete the **Sign-off** block. Then `/sdd:baseline` may seal v1.0.
- **Item counts:** FR **122** · NFR **80** · Epics **14** · Features **49** · Rules **57** · formerly-flagged items now Approved **57**.
