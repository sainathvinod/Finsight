# ADR-010 — AI Masking Policy (Deterministic Irreversible Redaction)

- **Status:** Accepted · **Date:** 2026-07-24 · accepted as part of the authorized AI Platform build (Phase 4C)
- **Deciders:** Technical Lead / Software Architect (decided); product owner (approved the AI Platform roadmap and the pre-implementation supersession of the FR-098/NFR-016 token-map language)
- **Feature/plan:** AI Platform (Phase 4C). Governs the `MaskingService` masking seam that runs before prompt construction. Preserves ADR-002 layering, ADR-003 determinism, the deterministic/AI boundary, and the FinSight *Trust > AI* principle.

## 1. Status
Accepted. This ADR is the defining decision record for how FinSight masks AI-bound content. It records the **authorized pre-implementation supersession** of the earlier reversible-tokenization language in FR-098 and NFR-016: the decision to mask was made and approved *before* the `MaskingService` was written; the implementation did not independently redefine the requirements. It is distinct from ADR-009, which governs persistence and retention and explicitly deferred masking to "Phase 4C+"; this ADR realizes that deferral.

## 2. Context
When AI is enabled, Fin explains deterministic outputs over minimal, already-verified grounding (§3.2 of `10-ai-and-agents.md`). That grounding can quote potentially sensitive content. Egress must be minimized and maskable before transmission (FR-098, NFR-016, NFR-015). The earlier requirement wording envisioned **reversible tokenization** of vendor/resource names, amounts, and identifiers with an in-application token map. On review, that model was rejected for FinSight because (a) a retained token map is itself a sensitive artifact and a leakage/retention risk (ADR-009 has no encryption-at-rest yet), and (b) masking financial values (amounts, GL/accounting accounts, invoice numbers) would corrupt the very deterministic facts Fin exists to explain, breaking financial-fact fidelity. The masking scope was therefore re-aimed at **personal and contact identifiers**, using **irreversible redaction with no token map**.

## 3. Decision
- **Deterministic irreversible redaction, not reversible tokenization.** Recognized values are replaced with fixed, category-specific tokens (e.g. `[REDACTED_EMAIL]`). Redaction is one-way; the original value cannot be recovered from the output.
- **Approved masking categories.** Configured employee names, email addresses, phone numbers, tax identifiers (SSN/EIN and labeled TINs), banking identifiers (IBAN, labeled SWIFT/BIC, labeled routing/ABA, labeled bank-account numbers), and structured `actor` fields on audit events and review memory.
- **Categories intentionally NOT masked.** Deterministic financial facts are preserved verbatim: amounts, percentages, variances, GL/accounting account numbers, invoice numbers, findings, rules, source references, fingerprints, review identifiers, and vendor/resource names. These are the substance of a grounded explanation and are the deterministic engine's truth, not personal data.
- **Financial-fact fidelity rationale.** Masking a financial value would alter the fact Fin is explaining and could make a cited answer inconsistent with the deterministic record. Fidelity of financial facts outranks over-broad masking; the policy is deliberately biased toward *not* masking an ambiguous value rather than risk corrupting a financial fact.
- **No generic long-digit masking.** There is no rule that masks bare unlabeled digit runs. Sensitive numeric identifiers are recognized only by distinctive format (e.g. `###-##-####` for SSN) or by an explicit adjacent label (e.g. `routing number …`). An unlabeled digit run is left untouched so account numbers, amounts, and invoice numbers survive.
- **Structured actor masking.** The person-bearing `actor` field on audit events and review-memory entries is wholly redacted to `[REDACTED_ACTOR]` when `mask_actor_fields` is enabled (default on). When disabled, the actor value is still passed through the free-text category pipeline so a configured name or email there is not leaked.
- **Optional configured employee-name list.** Employee names are matched **only** against an explicitly configured list, exact and whitespace-normalized, case-insensitive. The list is empty by default and an empty list never blocks execution. FinSight does not attempt to discover unlisted names.
- **No NER, fuzzy matching, or ML.** Recognition is by explicit, compiled, deterministic patterns and exact configured-name matching only. No named-entity recognition, no fuzzy/approximate matching, no title-case inference, no machine learning.
- **No token map.** Nothing maps a token back to an original value. No reverse index, no lookup table, no correlation identifier is produced or stored.
- **No persistence of original values.** Masking is pure and in-memory: no I/O, no persistence, no network, and no retention of original values or hashes of them. Inputs are never mutated; new immutable copies are returned.
- **Deterministic precedence.** Patterns run in a fixed, documented order (already-redacted-token guard → email → IBAN → labeled SWIFT/routing/bank-account → tax → phone → configured employee names). Identical inputs produce byte-identical output. Applying masking to already-masked content is idempotent (a second pass changes nothing).
- **Fail-closed behavior.** Any masking error raises `MaskingError` and yields **no** partial result; the (future) provider must never be called on partially-masked content.
- **Policy versioning.** The masking policy carries an explicit, immutable, non-sensitive `MASKING_POLICY_VERSION` (`"v1"`), surfaced on the masking summary so a future interaction journal can record *which* policy version applied — as metadata only, never original values, hashes, or a token map.

## 4. Consequences
**Positive:** no retained sensitive artifact (no token map, no reversible mapping); financial facts reach Fin intact so grounded explanations stay faithful to the deterministic record; recognition is deterministic, auditable, and testable; fail-closed prevents partial-mask leakage.

**Negative / limitation (accepted, and enforced in code comments + this ADR):**
- **Redaction is irreversible** — Fin can never re-hydrate a masked value; it cannot refer to a masked person by name even where that would aid an explanation. This is intentional.
- **Locale/format coverage.** Patterns and labels are US/English-oriented (e.g. `###-##-####`, 10-digit phone, 9-digit routing, English labels; contiguous IBAN form only). International formats, non-English labels, and spaced/grouped identifiers may not be recognized.
- **Employee-name completeness depends on configuration.** A name not in the configured list is not masked. Because matching is on complete configured token sequences, a configured name that appears **inside a longer human name** (e.g. "Jane Smith" within "Mary Jane Smith") **will** be masked; the service does not and will not infer whether adjacent words form a larger name. This is a deterministic, documented limitation — resolving it correctly would require NER, which is explicitly excluded.
- **This masking is a minimization control, not a substitute for encryption-at-rest.** Per ADR-009, the AI Platform remains synthetic/demo-only until encryption-at-rest exists.

## 5. Verification
Covered by `tests/ai/test_masking.py`: category redaction and non-masking of financial facts; deterministic precedence; purity/non-mutation; byte-identical determinism; idempotence and reserved-token handling; policy version; provenance free-text masking with structural fields preserved; configured-name masking in normal prose without substring matches. `application/ai` imports no `sqlite3`/PySide6/`urllib` (architecture fitness test). A skipped placeholder test documents that future orchestration must pass only `MaskingResult` values to the PromptBuilder.

## 6. Traceability
Defines the masking policy referenced by **FR-098** (Masking (redaction) option) and **NFR-016** (Masking effectiveness), and aligns §3.3 of `specs/10-ai-and-agents.md`. Relationship to **ADR-009**: ADR-009 governs AI persistence, retention, and data classification and explicitly deferred masking to Phase 4C+; ADR-010 realizes that deferral and is the sole ADR defining the masking policy. ADR-009 remains authoritative for non-persistence of AI data. Preserves ADR-002 (layering), ADR-003 (determinism), and the deterministic/AI boundary.
